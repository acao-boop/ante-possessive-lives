Sources & Bibliography

Materials are organized by how they function in the project. Citations follow Chicago Notes and Bibliography conventions.

Conceptual &
Theoretical Framework

Texts used to define dispossession, racial capitalism, property formation, historical temporality, and ante-possessive critique.

Core framework · Theory

Nichols, Robert. Theft Is Property! Dispossession and Critical Theory. Durham, NC: Duke University Press, 2020.

Property formation · Theory / Cartography

Greer, Allan. Property and Dispossession: Natives, Empires and Land in Early Modern North America. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2018.

Historical temporality · Theory

Tomba, Massimiliano. Insurgent Universality: An Alternative Legacy of Modernity. New York: Oxford University Press, 2019.

Racial capitalism · Theory

Robinson, Cedric J. Black Marxism: The Making of the Black Radical Tradition. Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, 2000. Originally published 1983.

Racial capitalism / Black radical tradition · Theory

Al-Bulushi, Yousuf. “Thinking Racial Capitalism and Black Radicalism from Africa: An Intellectual Geography of Cedric Robinson’s World-System.” Geoforum 132 (June 2022): 252–262.

Primary project framework · Theory / Juan Patricio

Kazanjian, David. “Ante-Possession: A History of Dispossession’s Present.” American Literary History 34, no. 3 (2022): 863–892. https://doi.org/10.1093/alh/ajac091.

Primary project framework · Adam

Kazanjian, David. “Infrastructure in Black: An Ante-Commons in Colonial New England.” Social Text 41, no. 4 (157) (December 2023): 1–36. https://doi.org/10.1215/01642472-10909986.

Juan Patricio &
Colonial Yucatán

Historical and linguistic context for Black, Maya, and Spanish social relations in the peninsula.

Afro-Yucatecan social history · Juan Patricio

Restall, Matthew. The Black Middle: Africans, Mayas, and Spaniards in Colonial Yucatán. Stanford, CA: Stanford University Press, 2009.

Language / reducción · Juan Patricio

Hanks, William F. Converting Words: Maya in the Age of the Cross. Berkeley: University of California Press, 2010.

Adam &
Colonial New England

Historical context for Indigenous space, slavery, colonization, and the infrastructures surrounding Adam’s case.

Indigenous geography / relational space · Adam

Brooks, Lisa. The Common Pot: The Recovery of Native Space in the Northeast. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2008.

Indigenous histories / cartography · Adam

Brooks, Lisa. Our Beloved Kin: A New History of King Philip’s War. New Haven: Yale University Press, 2018.

Slavery and colonization · Adam

Warren, Wendy. New England Bound: Slavery and Colonization in Early America. New York: Liveright Publishing Corporation, 2016.

Archival Records

Adam · Antislavery print

Sewall, Samuel. The Selling of Joseph: A Memorial. Boston: Bartholomew Green and John Allen, 1700.

Adam · Proslavery response

Saffin, John. A Brief and Candid Answer to a Late Printed Sheet, Entituled, The Selling of Joseph. Boston, 1701. Reprinted in Notes on the History of Slavery in Massachusetts, edited by George H. Moore, 251–56. New York: D. Appleton, 1866.

Adam · Diary context

Sewall, Samuel. Diary of Samuel Sewall, 1674–1729. Edited by M. Halsey Thomas. 2 vols. New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 1973.

Adam · Municipal records, 1708 / 1714

Whitmore, William H., and William S. Appleton, eds. A Report of the Record Commissioners of the City of Boston, Containing the Records of Boston Selectmen, 1701 to 1715. Boston: Rockwell and Churchill, 1884.
