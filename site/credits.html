About & Credits

The current site is the 2026 Penn Undergraduate Research Mentoring Program (PURM) iteration of Ante-Possessive Lives in the Americas, developed under the direction of Professor David Kazanjian and building on earlier collaborative work at Stanford.

Stanford Collaborators

Earlier iterations of Ante-Possessive Lives in the Americas were developed by Professor Kazanjian in collaboration with Stanford student researchers Stella Jingyi Li and Lizbeth Luevano, thanks to the Stanford Humanities Center. The current site builds upon that earlier digital work.
