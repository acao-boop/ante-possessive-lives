## External interactive media

The Knight Lab StoryMaps are embedded from external URLs and are not stored in this repository. Their project-specific credits and fallback links appear on the corresponding case pages. Knight Lab, Leaflet, OpenStreetMap, and any embedded media retain their own attribution and terms.

## Provenance rule

Do not invent a creator, collection, date, license, or scan identifier when the project record is incomplete. Retain the qualified description, locate a better source record, or replace the image with a verifiable alternative.
