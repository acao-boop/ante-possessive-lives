# V25 Theory Expansion — Review Notes

This review build preserves the current Adam Saffin and Juan Patricio case prose. Their only V25 change is the shared alignment and stylesheet version update.

## Theory visuals

1. **From Theft to Recursive Dispossession** appears in **Dispossession**. It contrasts the familiar account of theft with a recursive account in which dispossession helps produce property.
2. **Ante-Possession: 3 Relations to Ownership** appears under **A Central Question**. It presents ante-possession as before, against, and apposite to ownership.
3. **Recursive Dispossession: How the Cycle Expands** appears in **Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade**. Its evenly spaced cycle is Capture → Propertization → Capitalized → Expansion.
4. **The Maní Land Treaty Map (1557): Space as Relation** appears in **Cartography**. It uses the full-resolution screenshot supplied for this project.

All diagrams are native HTML/CSS, use the existing paper, ink, rule, and seal-wax palette, and collapse into a vertical reading order on narrow screens.

## Review convention

Only proposed prose expansions appear in red (`.review-addition`). Diagram titles, diagram text, captions, and source credits remain in the normal site palette.

## Map citation

Gaspar Antonio Chi, *The Maní Land Treaty Map*, 1557. Courtesy of The Latin American Library, Tulane University.

## Page-title alignment

The H1 titles on Theory & Method, Adam Saffin, and Juan Patricio now share the left edge of the main prose column on desktop and retain the existing mobile layout.
